Declickbait: End-to-End System Evaluation Under a Strict 35M Parameter Ceiling
Abstract—Clickbait headlines in contemporary digital news and video platforms deliberately exploit curios-
ity withholding and emotional sensationalism to artificially inflate click-through rates. In South Asian digital
spheres characterized by tri-modal linguistic diversity—spanning Standard Bengali (বাংলা), Romanized Bengali
(Banglish), and English—detecting clickbait and synthesizing truthful, fact-grounded replacement headlines
presents a profound computational challenge. While large pretrained transformer models (e.g., BanglishBERT,
110M parameters) achieve competitive classification accuracy, their computational cost, excessive VRAM
footprint (> 2.2 GB), and lack of generative remediation capabilities preclude real-time client-side or edge
deployment. In this research, we enforce a strict sub-35M parameter ceilingand present the systematic ar-
chitectural evolution, distributed training methodology, empirical benchmarking, and production deployment
of an end-to-end declickbaiting system. We design, train, and benchmark five distinct production neural archi-
tectures under a standardized prefix codename taxonomy: (i) cls_2t_bpe (Model 02_v3 Dual T4), a 3.59M-
parameter Two-Tower Byte-Level BPE Transformer Classifier with Title-Guided Evidence Cross-Attention
achieving 85.19% Macro-F1 and executing in 7.8 ms on CPU; (ii) joint_2t_wp (Model 11v2), a 16.23M-
parameter decoupled multi-head network combining discriminative classification (85.76% Macro-F1, 0.9363
ROC-AUC, τ ∗ = 0.60) and generative headline rewriting over shared BUET WordPiece representations; and
(iii) three specialized subword Pointer-Generator headline remediators (gen_ptr_best, gen_ptr_cov, and
gen_ptr_scratch, 14.16M parameters) equipped with Bahdanau attention and coverage mechanisms. Fur-
thermore, we establish the explicit architectural selection rationale for each system in our production portfolio,
detailing the concrete inductive biases, cross-attention formulations, and optimization constraints that make
each model optimal for its designated operational tier. All evaluations are computed live directly from trained
weights, validating that our autonomous gating policy preserves genuine informative articles unaltered while
routing sensational headlines to factual pointer-generator remediation.

1 Introduction and Theoretical Problem Formulation
Digital journalism and video aggregation platforms increasingly deploy deceptive, curiosity-inducing headlines to
artificially inflate click-through rates (CTR). These formulations systematically withhold critical entities (e.g.,“The
shocking truth you never expected” ), exaggerate emotional stakes, or make factual assertions directly contradicted
or unsupported by the article body. In South Asian digital environments, this challenge is exacerbated by code-
switching across three linguistic modalities:
1. StandardBengali( বাংলা): A morphologically rich, highly inflected Indo-Aryan language written in Eastern
Nagari script.
2. RomanizedBengali( Banglish): Phonetically transcribed Bengali rendered in ASCII Latin characters with-
out standardized orthography (e.g., “khobor”, “khbr”, “khoboor”).
3. Standard English: Standard journalistic and colloquial web discourse.
1.1 The Sub-35M Parameter Design Constraint
Existing commercial state-of-the-art NLP systems rely on massive pre-trained language models ( > 100M to 7B+
parameters). However, deploying such architectures for real-time web browsing (e.g., browser extensions, client-
side newsfeed sanitizers) incurs severe latency ( > 300 ms) and memory overhead ( > 2 GB VRAM), rendering
continuous client-side inference impossible.
To bridge the gap between academic accuracy and practical edge deployability, this investigation enforces a strict
architectural boundary:alldeployedcandidatemodelsmuststrictlycontainfewerthan35millionparameters
(< 35M). Under this constraint, models must fit comfortably within standard CPU RAM ( < 1.2 GB RSS) and
execute forward-pass classification in under 40 ms on commodity consumer CPUs.

2.2 Rationale for the 25k Stratified Dataset Construction
Training compact neural models directly on the uncurated 370k raw pool presents fatal statistical and computational
pitfalls:
• Modality Skew: BaitBuster accounts for 68.3% of the raw pool, while Romanized Banglish constitutes less
than 0.05%. Naive uniform sampling induces catastrophic representational collapse toward Bengali video
transcripts while completely failing on code-mixed and English news text.
• Class Imbalance: Non-clickbait articles outnumber clickbait by over 2 : 1 , biasing model posteriors toward
negative predictions.
• Hardware Constraints: Processing 370k samples with 768-token context windows exceeds the 12-hour
execution limit of free cloud GPUs (Google Colab and Kaggle T4x2).
To guarantee strict linguistic balance, class parity, and rapid convergence, we established a curated 25k Strat-
ified Dataset(24, 936 verified samples), split 80/10/10 into Dtrain = 19 , 948, Dval = 2 , 494, and Dtest = 2 , 494
samples (Table 2):
1. 100% Banglish Retention: All 131 verified Romanized Banglish samples ( 101 train, 14 val, 16 test) were
preserved.
2. Tri-Modal Language Parity: The remaining budget was partitioned equally: Bengali ( 8, 255, 33.1%),
Mixed (8, 288, 33.2%), and English (8, 262, 33.1%).
3. Exact50/50ClassEquilibrium : The overall dataset contains exactly49.89% clickbait (12, 439) and 50.11%
non-clickbait (12, 497).
4. Context Length Invariance: Minimum context lengths were enforced ( ≥ 350 characters for news; ≥ 80
characters for Banglish).

2.3 Ground-Truth Gap-Filling via Synthetic LLM Teacher Supervision
A critical challenge in clickbait remediation is that raw clickbait datasets (e.g., BanglaBait [ 2], Webis-17 [ 4],
BaitBuster-Bangla [1]) supply only binary labels and misleading titles, with no reference truthful replacement
headline (Y ∗) available to train seq2seq models.
To resolve this deficiency, we engineered a high-throughput teacher LLM pipeline that generated6,338verified
factual replacement titles, permanently serialized in data/cache/gemini_cache.jsonl:
• Teacher Model Routing: High-throughput synthesis was driven by opencode/muse-spark-1.3 (2, 598
samples) and muse-spark-1.2 (2, 028 samples). Culturally complex instances were routed to Google’s
gemini-3.1-flash-lite (742 samples) and gemini-3.5-flash-lite (186 samples). Fault-tolerant
fallback utilized z-ai/glm-5.3-flash (137), nex-agi/nex-n2.5-pro (42), and orcarouter (26).
• Chain-of-Thought Verification: Teachers were constrained to emit structured JSON: {"reasoning":
"...", "actual_title": "..."} , forcing explicit factual cross-referencing with the body text.
• Linguistic Invariance & Length Regularization: Candidate outputs were strictly validated via automated
quality filters (is_valid_generated_title), stripping quotes, emojis, and identical echoes of the clickbait
title, constraining output length to 4–16 factual words.

Architectural Justification forcls_2t_bpe (Model 02_v3 Dual T4): The selection of the 3.59M-parameter
Two-Tower Byte-BPE architecture for edge classification rests on three critical engineering justifications:
1. Byte-LevelBPERobustness : Standard WordPiece vocabularies often suffer catastrophic subword fragmen-
tation on phonetically irregular Romanized Banglish (e.g., “shobai shock” ) and colloquial slang. The 16,000
Byte-Level BPE tokenizer guarantees complete character-level fallback with zero out-of-vocabulary (OOV)
tokens, while keeping the embedding table compact ( 16, 000 × 128 = 2 .05M parameters, vs. > 4.1M in
32k vocabularies).
2. AsymmetricalTwo-TowerInductiveBias : Single-stream models concatenate title and body ([CLS ]T [SEP ]C),
causing short sensational titles (< 15 tokens) to be diluted by long article bodies ( > 500 tokens). The asym-
metrical Two-Tower design (3 Title layers vs. 4 Content layers) forces title representations to explicitly query
the entire body via cross-attention, preventing curiosity teasers from evading detection.
3. Direct Unsupported Bait Detection: By computing the token-level minimum-maximum cosine similarity
(Smin) between title tokens and all context sentence representations, the architecture directly operational-
izes the linguistic definition of clickbait: identifying when headline entities have zero factual support in the
accompanying article.

3.3 Model 11v2 (joint_2t_wp): Decoupled Multi-Head Architecture (16.23M Params)
Model 11v2 (standardized codenamejoint_2t_wp) unites the discriminative Two-Tower classification mechanism
and the autoregressive pointer-generator decoder over shared 32,000 BUET WordPiece embeddings (Figure 3).
• Decoupled Multi-Task Optimization: To resolve the gradient scale disparity where autoregressive se-
quence loss gradients ( ∥ggen∥ ≈ 1.85) drown out classification gradients ( ∥gcls∥ ≈ 0.12), learning rates
are partitioned into separate AdamW optimizer parameter groups:
lrcls = 8 × 10−5 (Title/Content Transformers & Cross-Attention Head) (5)
lrgen = 5 × 10−4 (BiLSTM Encoder, Pointer Decoder, & Attention Gate) (6)
with independent Cosine Annealing schedules, enabling simultaneous convergence of both heads without
destructive gradient competition.
• Decoupled CheckpointSerialization: Rather than coupling weights into a single compromise epoch, train-
ing serializes two optimal checkpoints:
1. checkpoint_cls_best.pt: Attained atEpoch15 with85.76%ValMacro-F1 ,0.9363ROC-AUC ,
and balanced decision threshold τ ∗ = 0.60.
2. checkpoint_gen_best.pt: Attained at Epoch 38 as autoregressive refinement lowers validation
generation loss to 4.6201 and validation perplexity to101.50.

production due to destructive gradient interference when disparate loss objectives are naively summed. Injoint_2t_wp
(Model 11v2), the classification head (BCEWithLogitsLoss) and generation head (CrossEntropyLoss with dynamic
pointer distributions) exhibit starkly contrasting convergence trajectories:

6.3 The Gradient Scale Disparity and Decoupled Multi-Task Training
During joint multi-task training in Model 10, backpropagation combines classification cross-entropy and autore-
gressive sequence generation:
Ltotal = Lcls(y, ˆy) + Lgen(Y, ˆY) + λcovLcov (7)
Empirical gradient logging revealed:
• Classification loss Lcls ≈ 0.35 produces gradient norm ∥gcls∥ ≈ 0.12.
• Autoregressive sequence loss accumulates over 30–64 steps:Lgen ≈ 4.50, producing gradient norm∥ggen∥ ≈
1.85.
Because ∥ggen∥ ≈ 15 × ∥gcls∥, naive shared updates caused classification gradients to be overwhelmed. In
joint_2t_wp (Model 11v2), this was resolved by decoupling the optimization rates: setting lrcls = 8 × 10−5
and lrgen = 5 × 10−4, allowing the classification towers to converge stably without destabilization.
6.4 Salient Context Selection vs. Blind Lead Truncation & Pronoun Resolution
Standard summarization baselines frequently rely on blind lead truncation (e.g., clean_body_lead[:180]), ex-
ploiting the journalistic “inverted pyramid” structure where core facts reside in the opening lines. However, our
qualitative investigation revealed a critical vulnerability in extended feature journalism (exemplified by Case 5, an
Ayushmann Khurrana profile):
1. The Anecdotal Narrative Trap: In long-form journalism, introductory paragraphs often detail anecdotal
color (e.g., a dinner story with 250 college students) rather than the article’s investigative subject. Truncating
blindly to the first 180 tokens rendered the true factual revelation—located in Paragraph 5 at token index
∼ 380—completely invisible to the sequence generator.
2. Forced Subword Hallucination: Constrained beam search with rigid minimum length (min_len = 11 )
forced the model to extract arbitrary subwords from the irrelevant opening anecdote, producing fragmented
disfigurations (e.g.,শজনকেলজিশক্ষাথীর্েহােটেলআরৈতিররােতরখাবােরর ).
3. Unicode NFKC Disintegration: Raw Bengali text pasted from diverse web CMS platforms frequently con-
tains decomposed Unicode codepoints (such as য় represented as single character U+09DF vs. consonant
plus nukta U+09AF+U+09BC). BUET BanglishBERT’s WordPiece tokenizer treats unnormalized variants as
[UNK] (ID 1). Applying NFKC normalization resolved every token without exception:কঁািরয়ার → [কঁািরয়ার,
##ে◌],েপেয়েছন → [েপেয়েছন], andআয়ুষ্মান → [আয়ু, ##ষ্, ##মান], completely eliminating out-of-vocabulary
degradation.
4. Salient Context Selection & Pronoun-Masked Remediation: We resolved this structural limitation by
implementing Salient Context Selection: computing lexical overlap between headline keywords and all article
sentences to elevate high-evidence sentences alongside the article lead. Furthermore, for pronoun-withholding

6.5 SubwordRepresentationEfficiency: TheoreticalSuperiorityofthe3.59MTwo-TowerDesign
A central theoretical takeaway of this research is why our lightweight 3.59M Two-Tower architecture (cls_2t_bpe)
delivers high-accuracy detection on colloquial and code-mixed clickbait while maintaining extreme edge efficiency:
1. Compact Domain Adaptation: Rather than relying on generic out-of-domain pretraining, cls_2t_bpe
trains its 16,000 Byte-Level BPE vocabulary end-to-end on culturally nuanced video and news discourse.
This ensures that colloquial hyperbolic idioms and sensational framing patterns are mapped directly into
discriminative feature spaces without parameter bloat.
2. Inductive Cross-Attention Bias: The dedicated Unsupported Bait Detector ( Smin) explicitly computes the
minimum of maximum token-level cosine similarities across article sentences. Sensational terms unsupported
by the body exhibit strongly negative cosine scores (Smin ≪ 0), decisively driving the classification posterior
without requiring massive parameter counts.
3. Edge Computational Efficiency: Operating at only 14.37 MB RSS and 7.8 ms forward latency on CPU,
cls_2t_bpe proves that compact architectures equipped with domain-tailored cross-attention inductive bi-
ases can match or surpass massive general-purpose language models in practical deployment.
7 Conclusion and Future Perspectives
This research demonstrated the efficacy of high-accuracy, real-time multilingual declickbaiting under a strict 35M
parameter ceiling. Through the systematic design, training, and benchmarking of our standardized model suite
(cls_2t_bpe, joint_2t_wp, gen_ptr_best, gen_ptr_cov, and gen_ptr_scratch), we validated that:
• Two-Tower Evidence Cross-Attention matches or exceeds 110M pretrained transformers in discriminative
capability while executing in under 8 ms on CPU.
• Pointer-generator networks with Bahdanau coverage penalty effectively eliminate hallucinations and repetitive
stutters in Bengali text remediation.
• The autonomous threshold gate (τ ∗ = 0.60−0.70) cleanly isolates genuine articles, preserving them unaltered
while remediating deceptive headlines.
